<h2>Description</h2>
The evidence discussed comes from the computer of Robert Hunter, who was
accused of kidnapping someone. I was asked by the judge to analyze the hard disk for
evidence of kidnapping or any other crimes at all. A timeline of events will be discussed,
and all evidence will have proof to back up what is said. Mr. Hunter also claims that
everything put on his computer that may be illegal was put there by someone else or the
result of a computer virus. He also claims the files were corrupted by police when they
seized the machine as it was running. All these concerns will be addressed inside of the
full report. For this case, the FTK suite of tools was used for examination of evidence.

<h2>Executive Summary:</h2>
On the hard disk of Bob Hunter, there was reasonable evidence found regarding the
stalking, kidnapping and attempted blackmail of two people and their fathers. Provided
below will be a summary of the findings in this report, for more detailed explanations, refer
to the appropriate sections as listed in the table of contents.

- On the hard disks, there are many images found that were taken on a Canon
PowerShot G2 model camera in April of 2002 that are all pictures of the two
kidnapped people in question, Christina and Sabrina. All the images found to be
taken by this camera were of these two.

- Bob had accessed many of these images in his recent folder, which provides a list
of all the files he has recently opened on his computer.

- Inside Bob’s email, messages can be found between an accomplice by the name of
Billy Ray. Through the email exchanges between Bob and Billy, they detail their
plans to make a website with images of Christina and Sabrina and use the website
to try and blackmail their fathers into paying them money for the safe return of their
daughters.

- The website in question was visited by Bob on 06/03/2002 and can be seen to have
had the same images taken by the camera embedded into the website. Bob also
visited websites to learn about stalking and information on doing it.

- There was a Yahoo Message log between Bob and Billy where it is stated that they
may have been found out and moved the evidence to external devices and were
going to attempt to wipe this drive to hide any evidence. This was followed by
internet searches for terms like “disk wiping” and “bc wipe”, which ended up in Bob
browsing to a website that distributed a program called BCwipe, which said it was a
program that could safely and securely delete files on a system.
